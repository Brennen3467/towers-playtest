# Load-bearing sources

*Curated from the URLs cited in lane reports 01–15 and verifier reports verify-1 to verify-5 (research date 2026-10-01). No new sources were added. Each note says what the source was used to establish. Where a verifier disputed or corrected a source, the note says so.*

## 1. Legal and regulatory

### Securities, brokerage and licensing
- https://www.ibba.org/articles/the-state-line-trap/ — Only ~23 states have an M&A broker exemption, and the federal exemption doesn't pre-empt them. About 17 states require a real-estate licence for business brokerage.
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/78o — Exchange Act §15(b)(13) M&A broker exemption text (<$25M EBITDA / <$250M revenue). It places no restriction on the form of fee.
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/78cc — §29(b): contracts made in violation of the Act are void, so an unregistered finder's fee can be clawed back.
- https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/Chapter475/All — Florida real-estate licence required to broker business opportunities. No commission sharing with unlicensed referrers (business, liquor-licence and restaurant brokerage).

### Customs and trade
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/19/111.36 — Customs brokers may not let fees for customs business, including drawback, "inure to the benefit of" unlicensed persons.
- https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/H276784 — CBP refused a broker's plan to pay referral fees tied to signed clients. This killed the drawback contingency split (verify-1).
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/19/103.31 — Vessel manifests are public, CBP sells AMS data "at production cost" (no published price), and importers can request free 2-year confidentiality.

### Email, calls and deceptive marketing
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business — CAN-SPAM has no B2B exemption. Penalties run up to $53,088 per non-compliant email.
- https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-400393A1.pdf — FCC Feb 2024 ruling that AI-generated voices are "artificial" under the TCPA, so consent is required. This ruled out AI cold-calling everywhere.
- https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/2223156-accessibe-inc — FTC's $1M order against accessiBe for claiming "full compliance". The precedent behind every "never promise compliance or AI outcomes" caveat.
- https://codes.findlaw.com/us/title-39-postal-service/39-usc-sect-3001 — Deceptive Mailings Prevention Act: mail that references a federal agency must carry "not a government document" notices (verify-4).

### Product-specific rules
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/27-CCR-25600.2 — Prop 65 retailer duty is narrow; the primary duty sits with manufacturers and importers. This killed the retailer-facing Prop 65 idea (verify-2).
- https://www.newsmediaalliance.org/newsom-signs-sb-690-into-law/ — SB 690, signed 30 Sep 2026, ends private CIPA pen-register website suits from 1 Jan 2027.
- https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/statutes/2025/119.071 — Florida public-records exemption for information revealing the firesafety systems of private property (fire ITM Overdue Feed).

### Professional practice, fee-splitting and recovery rules
- https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-11/subpart-B/subject-group-ECFRa9deb1f9b3dd8c7/section-11.14 — Only attorneys may represent others before the USPTO in trademark matters, so direct-to-owner filings are out.
- https://images.law.com/contrib/content/uploads/documents/399/77701/aba-formal-opinion-501.pdf — ABA Formal Op. 501: lawyers are responsible for how their lead generators solicit, so vendor templates become the subscriber's ethics exposure.
- https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/717.1322 — Florida bars anyone but an attorney, FL CPA or PI from taking compensation for helping an unclaimed-property claimant. This blocks the 50/50 CPA split.
- https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=5061 — California B&P 5061 bars CPAs from paying or taking referral fees and commissions (unclaimed property, §45B tip credit).
- https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._occ._code_section_1152.001 — Texas property-tax consultant registration, which reaches paid "assistants" (verify-3).

## 3. Data access (what is actually in the files)

### Licensing and insurance records
- https://data.texas.gov/api/views/7358-krk7.json — TX TDLR licence metadata: only an expiration date, no issue date. This broke the SuccessionMap tenure signal (verify-1, verify-5).
- https://www.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/dataportal/ContractorList — CA CSLB files include the WC carrier, policy number and dates. No GL data, and emails withheld by law.
- https://data.wa.gov/resource/ciwg-agsx.json — WA L&I contractor insurance: carrier, agency name and expiration, updated 3×/day. GL only; 89% at $1M limits.

### Trade data
- https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/files/2021066pap.pdf — Fed FEDS 2021-066. In BOL data, values are missing on 64–68% of records, HS codes are imputed, and consignee IDs are missing on 8–35%.
- https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/electronic-vessel-manifest-confidentiality — CBP's online manifest-confidentiality tool approves requests in about 24 hours, so targeted importers can go dark.

### Government audits and water
- https://www.fac.gov/api/dictionary/ — FAC API tables (general, findings, CAPs), including auditee email and phone.
- https://app.fac.gov/dissemination/search/ — FAC search counts: only 8,187 of 46,650 AY2024 audits have any finding (verify-3).
- https://github.com/Environmental-Policy-Innovation-Center/dw-dashboard — EPIC's open-source 50-state SRF/IUP normalization, so SRF lists are already free.

### Healthcare
- https://data.cms.gov/provider-characteristics/medicare-provider-supplier-enrollment/revalidation-due-date-list — Revalidation Due Date List: 2.94M rows, ~90% "TBD", and no address fields (verify-4).
- https://download.cms.gov/nppes/NPI_Files.html — NPPES weekly and monthly files: authorized official and phone, but no email.
- https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/mj5m-pzi6 — Doctors & Clinicians file: graduation year and group size. No ownership or revenue fields.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12631121/ — Health Affairs Scholar: 91.8% of TiC rates and 95.4% of provider–code pairs are ghost rates.

### Trucking
- https://data.transportation.gov/resource/c5y8-a4uz.json — Motus Insur file (post-May 2026) has no cancellation-date column, so future-dated lapses are gone from public bulk data.

### Florida property
- https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/condos-timeshares-mobile-homes/public-records/ — FL DBPR condo CSVs. The "managing entity" field is the association; the CAM firm appears only as a C/O line in 50.2% of rows.

### Inspections and stormwater
- https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/43nn-pn8j.json — NYC restaurant inspections: ~2-day lag and phone numbers, but no email.
- https://data.ca.gov/dataset/stormwater-regulatory-including-enforcement-actions-information-and-water-quality-results — CA SMARTS on data.ca.gov: 2.3M monitoring rows with a SQL API, better than the lane claimed.

### Trademarks
- https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2020/owner-email-address-field-now-masked-teas-and-teasi-documents-tsdr — USPTO masks owner email only when the owner has counsel, so pro-se emails stay public.

### Business records
- https://quintel.ai/blog/ucc-filing-search-by-state — UCC bulk access by state: from free (CO, VT, CT, WV) to $24K (AZ). NC and GA prohibit scraping.

## 5. Benchmarks: reply rates, churn, deliverability, AI measurement

### Cold-email reply rates
- https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026 — 2026 B2B cold-email average reply 3.43%, top quartile 5.5%. The most-cited baseline across lanes.
- https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates — Belkins (7.5M emails): 0.45% average reply, healthcare ≈0.56–0.6%. Basis for cutting lane 06's 2% conversion assumption.
- https://www.cleanlist.ai/blog/2026-02-18-cold-email-response-rate-statistics — Owners/founders reply at 0.57%, worse than the 1–3% assumed for M&A owner outreach.
- https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/cold-outbound-reply-rate-benchmarks/ — Construction/real estate median 5.0% reply, and trigger-led outreach claimed to roughly triple replies (vendor data, optimistic).
- https://www.cleverly.co/blog/cold-email-benchmarks-by-industry — Contrary dataset putting construction at 0.56–0.60%, which shows how wide the spread is.
- https://www.revenueflow.com/blog/manufacturing-cold-email-benchmarks — Manufacturing ~2–4% replies and 0.6–1.5% meetings.
- https://www.tebra.com/theintake/healthcare-reports/billing-companies/getting-paid-how-to-get-medical-billing-clients — 72% of medical billing firms cite referrals as their top source of new business, so they don't run outbound.

### Churn and retention
- https://www.kalungi.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks — SMB SaaS churn runs 3–7% per month.
- https://focus-digital.co/average-marketing-agency-churn/ — SEO agency clients churn ~3.2% per month (~32-month life).
- https://investors.yext.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/391/yext-announces-second-quarter-fiscal-2027-results — Yext customers under $50K ARR had 69% annual dollar gross retention (July 2026).

### Deliverability
- https://redsift.com/resources/blog/gmails-enforcement-ramps-up-what-bulk-senders-need-to-know — Gmail/Yahoo 0.3% spam-complaint ceiling (0.1% target). Gmail began rejecting non-compliant mail in Nov 2025.

### AI-answer measurement
- https://surferseo.com/blog/llm-scraped-ai-answers-vs-api-results — LLM API answers overlap the consumer UI only 15.5–23.8%, so "ChatGPT doesn't recommend you" proofs built from the API are unreliable.
- https://searchengineland.com/ai-recommendation-lists-rarely-repeat-study-468076 — Fewer than 1 in 100 AI recommendation runs return the same brand list, so a "rank" is noise.
